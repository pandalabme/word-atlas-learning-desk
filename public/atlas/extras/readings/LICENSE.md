# Reading corpus license and provenance

The JSON files in this directory whose IDs begin with `local-` were created for
the `pandalabme/electronic_diy` Word Atlas Learning Desk on 2026-10-29. Their
English passages, Chinese translations and comprehension questions were
generated with an OpenAI model from short topic and vocabulary prompts and then
reviewed for vocabulary markers, translation alignment and answer consistency.
They were not copied from `0xlei.tech/words/atlas/extras/readings/`.

The `ids` values and bare English headwords refer to the Word Atlas dataset,
which is licensed CC BY 4.0 by Word Atlas contributors. No upstream hosted
reading passage is included here.
